Meet Our Team

A multidisciplinary group of clinical psychologists, research staff, graduate students, and undergraduate researchers united around eating disorder science.

CL
Director

Dr. Cheri A. Levinson, Ph.D.

Professor of Psychological & Brain Sciences

Dr. Levinson's research develops personalized, evidence-based treatments for eating disorders and the anxiety that so often accompanies them. Her work has been funded by NIMH, NIH, and multiple foundations, and has been featured in the Sydney Morning Herald, APS Observer, and more.
